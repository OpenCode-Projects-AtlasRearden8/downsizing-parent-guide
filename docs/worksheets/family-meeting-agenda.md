# Family-Meeting Agenda (Printable)

> One printed page per meeting. 60 minutes, timer on the table. Parent's chair at the head of the table. Also bring: this manual's [fair-talk scripts](../01-family-meeting.md), a copy of any prior meeting's decisions, and the stop-signal word everyone chose.

```
MEETING DATE: ____/____/____      LOCATION: _______________________
PRESENT: (parent first, then everyone present)
  1. ____________________   2. ____________________
  3. ____________________   4. ____________________
STOP SIGNAL WORD (agreed): ______________________
```
